# Garlic Bread

Simple but effective garlic bread recipe. 

Taken from https://www.ambitiouskitchen.com/the-best-garlic-bread-recipe/ 

## Ingredients

There isn't much to the recipe, so getting fresh ingredients makes a big difference.

- Fresh Bread (preferred French Bread)
- Butter (preferred Kerrigold salted butter)
- Fresh garlic
- Fresh Parsley
- Optional: Greated Parmesian Cheese

## Instructions

Ingredient portions are for about half a loaf of French Bread. Update proportions accordingly.

1. In a bowl, mix together about half a block of kerrigold butter, 6 cloves of garlic, and a handful of parsley leaves.
    - Slightly melt the butter in a microwave if needed, about 5s at a time. It should be soft but not completely melted. 
2. Cut the bread into 4-6in loaves, and then cut each loaf in half length-wise.
3. Spread the butter mix on top of the bread. It should be a healthy spread. 
4. Toast the bread in the oven at 425 F for 10-15 minutes. 
    - If adding parmesian cheese, sprinkle it on top with about 2 minutes left to cook.
